## 1. Why the governance model exists

APZHUB needed more than product engineering excellence. It needed a portfolio-wide way to:

- promote proven practices without copying product-specific documents;
- separate **what exists** (Catalogue) from **what is mandatory** (Baseline);
- require independent technical and board approvals (Dual Approval);
- declare a stable operating baseline and then **evolve** it safely;
- freeze the **process** so ordinary standard promotions cannot redesign governance.

The model exists so future architects inherit a durable engineering organisation, not a collection of one-off documents.

## 6. Principles that shaped the organisation

1. **Abstraction, not duplication** — enterprise standards are generalised from proven practice.
2. **Catalogue ≠ Baseline** — inventory vs mandatory set.
3. **Dual Approval** — Architecture Review + Product Board; neither substitutes for the other.
4. **Reference implementation** — APZQEP proves; APZHUB owns enterprise rules.
5. **Stable Baseline Policy** — after STABLE, new standards are enhancements.
6. **Governance Process Freeze** — process changes need a governance programme and version bump.
7. **Enterprise Enhancement Policy** — new standards extend capability without redefining it.
8. **Two version axes** — Governance Version (how) vs Baseline Version (what).

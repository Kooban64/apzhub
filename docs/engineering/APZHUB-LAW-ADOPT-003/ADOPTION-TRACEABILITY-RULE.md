## Rule

Every enterprise adoption engineering programme must be **traceability-driven**.

No code change may be made unless it can be traced to:

1. an approved assessment gap, or
2. a Product Board decision, or
3. an authorised backlog item.

## Rationale

Keeps adoption disciplined and prevents products from accumulating opportunistic changes while they are being aligned to the enterprise model.

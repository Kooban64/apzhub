## Principles

1. Platform provides audit, AuthZ, and SoR boundaries — organisation overlays local law.
2. Evidence packs under `docs/releases/` are compliance artefacts for baseline claims.
3. Do not claim SOC2/ISO/etc. unless separately evidenced.
4. PRWL Known Limitations are compliance-relevant disclosures.

## Compliance Lead duties

- Retention schedules for audit/logs
- Access review cadence with Security Committee
- Vendor data processing inventory (engines)
- Review of customer-facing claims vs KL

## Vendor / supplier compliance

OSS adapters remain CE/self-hosted first; Enterprise Edition dependencies require Owner Approval.

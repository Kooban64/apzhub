## Product ownership

Each commercial APZ product has a **Product Owner** accountable for:

- User-facing scope honesty (PRWL / KL)
- Roadmap proposals (Owner Approval still required for delivery)
- Persona / GTM alignment
- Product SemVer evidence packs

## Programme vs project

| Mode                           | Use                                                      |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Named **Programme**            | Owner-approved APZHUB-* / APZ-* delivery with Acceptance |
| **Project** (within programme) | Time-boxed workstream; cannot exceed programme STOP      |

## Development lifecycle (enterprise view)

Governed by Engineering Operating Model + Platform Delivery Standard — not reinvented here.

## Product Council

Cross-product conflicts (naming, shared UX, event contracts) escalate to Product Council → Owner if SemVer/STOP impacted.

## Forbidden

Product teams must not: call engines directly; invent notify/automation subsystems; claim Email SoR / execute / FIN-001.

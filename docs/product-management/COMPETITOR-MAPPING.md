# APZHUB Competitor Mapping

> **Programme:** APZHUB-PRODUCT-MANAGEMENT-001  
> **Classification:** DOCUMENTATION ONLY — framing only  
> **Date:** 2026-07-19  
> **Rule:** Competitors are market categories; APZHUB does not copy proprietary products.

---

## Purpose

Map APZHUB products to **market categories** and illustrative competitor classes for positioning. Not a feature-parity matrix and not a recommendation to implement parity.

---

## Platform-level framing

| APZHUB                               | Competes with (class)                           | Differentiation                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| APZHUB Enterprise Operating Platform | Fragmented point-tool stacks; generic “portals” | Unified Workbench · platform services · brand-masked OSS engines · self-hosted first |

---

## Competitive rules

1. Never market engine brands as the product.
2. Never claim feature parity without certification evidence.
3. Prefer “replace tool sprawl” narrative over single-point rival bashing.
4. Law Platform positioning emphasises practice operations + trust + platform suite adjacency.

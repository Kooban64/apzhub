## Purpose

Authoritative commercial product management reference for every APZ product: positioning, editions, licensing models, pricing principles, feature/roadmap governance, personas, KPIs, and go-to-market.

**No implementation** of products, pricing engines, or licensing enforcement is authorised by this framework.

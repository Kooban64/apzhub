## Principle

Finish products with a repeatable lifecycle. Do not invent a new way to build each product.

**Protect the inventory.** Ideas will come. Do not let them grow. Finish. Then move on.

Prerequisite before Engineering Execution: [APZHUB-PORTFOLIO-OPERATING-PRINCIPLE.md](./APZHUB-PORTFOLIO-OPERATING-PRINCIPLE.md) — finite closeout inventory (four questions).

---

## Lifecycle (do not change)

1. **Assess** — What exists? What is missing? What does Production Ready mean? What is the shortest path?
2. **Accept a finite inventory** — No open-ended backlogs. No “while we’re here…” features. No architecture rewrites.
3. **Engineer continuously** — Remove inventory item by item. Report only Closed / In Progress / Remaining.
4. **Production Readiness** — Durability. Migration. Certification.
5. **Hardening** — Accessibility. Security. Performance. Regression. Operational readiness.
6. **Release Candidate**
7. **Production Ready**
8. **Operational Learning**

---

## Role of engineering execution

During Engineering Execution, work is implement → verify → migrate → certify → harden → release — not open-ended architecture consultation.

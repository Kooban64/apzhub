## Principle

**No APZHUB product enters Engineering Execution without a finite closeout inventory.**

---

## Four questions (mandatory before coding)

Every product must answer, in writing, before Engineering Execution opens:

1. **What already exists?**
2. **What is missing?**
3. **What defines Production Ready?**
4. **What is the shortest path to get there?**

Until those answers form a finite inventory with exit criteria, work stays in assessment / documentation only.

---

## Why

This is the decision that moved APZ Projects and APZQEP from ongoing programmes into finished Production Ready products — and that keeps the portfolio out of endless redesign.

Proven delivery model:

```text
Finite inventory → Continuous engineering → Production readiness
→ Hardening → Release Candidate → Production Ready → Operational Learning
```

---

## Explicitly forbidden without inventory

- Architecture redesign
- New frameworks
- Product Bible rewrite (unless genuinely missing)
- Open-ended “build the product” execution

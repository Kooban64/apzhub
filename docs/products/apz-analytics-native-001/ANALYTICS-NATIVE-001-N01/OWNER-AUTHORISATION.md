## Strategic objective

Determine whether APZ Analytics delivers a native APZHUB **Enterprise Decision Support** experience — questions and decisions first — while consuming SoRs and never becoming one.

Identify gaps only. Do not implement solutions.

## Audit areas (mandatory)

1. Native Experience
2. Engine Leakage
3. Measurement vs Decision framing

7. Relationship to RI #001–#005
8. Enterprise Capability Alignment

## Central questions

> When a user opens Analytics, do they start with a **business question / decision** — or with a dashboard catalogue?

> Does the product improve decisions, or only measure activity?

Expected long-term answer: **questions and decisions first**; dashboards are presentations only.

## Rule

User-facing APZ Analytics **identity** uses **questions · decisions · insights** language.

Product identity: **Decision Companion** — where better decisions are made.

Dashboards, reports, and datasets may exist below the product boundary as presentation/answer mechanics. They must not define the default product identity or Activity Bar metaphor.

## N-02 enforcement

Default Tenant Member identity reaches **insight** surfaces via `analytics.view`.  
**Presentation assets** (datasets, reports) and **operator** (health, diagnostics) require elevated grants — not the default decision-entry identity.

## N-03 enforcement

Primary experience is **Question → Insight → Decision**.  
Home, horizons, and Enterprise Question Catalogue are the entry. Suite paths are insight answers only.

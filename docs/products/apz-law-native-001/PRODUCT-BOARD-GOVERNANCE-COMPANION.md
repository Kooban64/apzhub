## Governance Companion Principle

> **APZ Law exists to help every product operate within enterprise obligations. It is not a practice management system.**

| Prefer (product identity)                     | Avoid (below boundary / removed from primary UX) |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Policy · Obligation · Compliance              | Matters · Practice · Firm operations             |
| Governance · Approval · Risk                  | Legal administration as product metaphor         |
| Evidence · Retention · Regulatory requirement | Trust / Billing as product identity              |

## Governance in Context

> **Users should encounter governance at the moment they perform work, not only when they enter APZ Law.**

Complements [Governance Context](../apzlaw/PRODUCT-BOARD-ENTERPRISE-GOVERNANCE.md) (appear where work is performed). N-02 establishes identity and RBAC; full contextual surfaces remain **N-03**.

## Directive (N-02)

Identity shall express **enterprise governance**.

Any vocabulary, navigation, or experience that implies law-firm practice management belongs **below the product boundary** or shall be removed from the primary user experience.

## Anti-pattern

Do **not** turn APZ Law into a compliance checklist that only says “you forgot regulation X.”

Prefer:

> These obligations apply to this business activity.

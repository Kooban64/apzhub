## Classification

Engineering · Architecture **FROZEN** · Lane 1 **UNCHANGED** · Playbook **UNCHANGED**

## Strategic objective

Transform APZ Workflow from an execution-oriented workspace into a **business process companion**.

Users understand the product entirely in terms of business outcomes. They never need to understand execution mechanics.

## Governing principles (already IN FORCE)

1. A workflow describes what the business intends to happen. It does not prescribe how technology makes it happen.
2. Users design a business process, not configure an automation engine.
3. Users compose outcomes; the platform determines execution.
4. Identity shall be expressed entirely in business process language.

## Scope

1. Business vocabulary
2. Activity Bar convergence (single business-oriented product)
3. Business Journey Catalogue
4. Native APZHUB experience (chrome, breadcrumbs, help, settings, onboarding, empty/loading/error)
5. Administrative separation (engine/ops secondary, role-gated)

## Explicitly out of scope

Automation execution · Provider integrations · Workflow engine redesign · Template execution logic · Scheduling enhancements · AI-assisted workflow generation · Lane 1 platform capabilities

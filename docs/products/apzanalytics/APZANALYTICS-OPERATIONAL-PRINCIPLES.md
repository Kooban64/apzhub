## Principles

1. **Analytics improves decisions; it does not replace decision-makers.**
2. **Analytics consumes Systems of Record; it never becomes one.**
3. **Questions precede visualisations.**
4. **Insights should always support an operational or strategic decision.**
5. **Administrative reporting remains operational tooling and does not define the product.**

| ID    | Outcome                                                 | Why it matters                                                            |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| BO-01 | Leaders answer performance questions in APZHUB          | Less reconstructing “how are we doing?” from exports and tribal knowledge |
| BO-02 | Decisions grounded in observed SoRs                     | Insight traces to Projects, Support, Time, Documents, Workflow, quality   |
| BO-03 | Earlier detection of risk and overload                  | Blocked work, at-risk projects, overloaded teams surface sooner           |
| BO-04 | Visible improvement over time                           | Delivery, SLA, and quality trends support management judgement            |
| BO-05 | Effort and delay understood without new SoRs            | Time and Workflow observations explain where work and wait concentrate    |
| BO-06 | Repeat-incident insight without owning Support data     | Support remains SoR; Analytics explains patterns                          |
| BO-07 | Portfolio insight across the operational business layer | One place to understand how the five RIs perform together                 |
| BO-08 | SoR discipline preserved                                | Analytics never becomes authoritative for operational truth               |
| BO-09 | Questions drive investment                              | Scope expands by decision need, not by chart catalogue                    |
| BO-10 | Stronger foundation for Law and future verticals        | Verticals consume insight without inventing parallel reporting stacks     |

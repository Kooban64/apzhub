# APZ Analytics — Product Vision

| Field     | Value             |
| --------- | ----------------- |
| Programme | APZ-ANALYTICS-000 |
| Status    | **APPROVED**      |
| Timestamp | 20260805T174500Z  |

## Near term (post mission agreement)

APZ Analytics is the unmistakable APZHUB place for enterprise performance questions — framed as Decision Support, never as a BI-engine console.

## Medium term

Priority enterprise questions (health, blockage, overload, SLA, risk, effort, delay, release safety) have trusted answer paths that observe RI SoRs.

## Longer term

Analytics becomes the memory of how work performs across the operational business layer. Law and future products consume insight. Continuous improvement of questions is routine. Visualisations remain servants of questions.

## Vision constraints

- Remain an **insight** product — do not become Projects, Support, Time, Documents, or Workflow.
- Remain **Decision Support**, not reporting branding.
- Never become a System of Record.
- Expand capability only with Owner Authorisation.
- Do not open Lane 1 platform programmes from this vision alone.

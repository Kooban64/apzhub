## Why organisations should use APZ Analytics

APZ Analytics is APZHUB’s Enterprise Decision Support: one trusted place to ask how the enterprise is performing, grounded in the operational business layer, without creating a second system of truth.

## Value pillars

| Pillar                 | Proposition                                                                   |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Questions**          | Leaders ask business questions, not “which chart?”                            |
| **Trust**              | Answers observe SoRs; conflict always resolves to operational products        |
| **Memory**             | Trends and interpretation across Time, Support, Projects, Documents, Workflow |
| **Discipline**         | Insight layer stays above the business — never becomes it                     |
| **Portfolio leverage** | Law and future verticals can rely on governed insight without parallel stacks |

## What we do not claim here

This face does not list dashboards, compare BI vendors, or describe how the product is built. Value is stated as **decision support benefit**.

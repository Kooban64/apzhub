## What is delivered

Permissioned users can enter APZ Analytics as Decision Companion, follow curated questions to insight evidence metadata on a fail-closed AnalyticsService → Metabase path, with honest limitation disclosure and Delivery-Standard hardening.

## Honest limitations (v1.0)

- Live visual embed is **not available** (metadata only).
- Analytics registry remains **in-memory MVP** (not Postgres SoR).
- Catalogue search filters titles/descriptions — not semantic search.
- AI / predictive analytics and product alerting are out of scope.

## Out of scope

Analytics 2.0, redesign, new engines, architecture reopen without ADR.

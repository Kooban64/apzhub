Outcomes are organisational results. They are not feature lists.

| ID    | Outcome                                                 | Why it matters                                                  |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| BO-01 | One trusted place for work documents                    | Teams stop reconstructing “where is the file?” across channels  |
| BO-02 | Clear ownership of documents and versions               | Accountability for currency and correctness                     |
| BO-03 | Controlled access to sensitive records                  | Permission replaces informal sharing risk                       |
| BO-04 | Faster discovery of the right document                  | Less time lost searching and asking colleagues                  |
| BO-05 | Reduced version confusion                               | Current version is identifiable; obsolete copies lose authority |
| BO-06 | Seamless linkage to Projects, Support, Time, and APZQEP | Documents attach to shared work context without tool sprawl     |
| BO-07 | Stronger compliance and retention posture               | Records practices become operational, not ad hoc                |
| BO-08 | Measurable document-related productivity                | Improvement decisions rest on evidence, not anecdote            |
| BO-09 | Clearer connection of documents to outcomes             | Artefacts support delivery, service, and quality decisions      |
| BO-10 | Stronger foundation for Workflow, Analytics, and Law    | Later products integrate around a mature document capability    |

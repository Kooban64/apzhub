## Mission statement

> **APZ Documents delivers enterprise document management as a native APZHUB business capability — capture, organisation, discovery, lifecycle, governance, compliance, and collaboration — so every product can attach trustworthy documents to real work without users ever leaving the APZHUB experience or seeing an implementation engine.**

## Product purpose

APZ Documents exists as the **Document Management** enterprise capability of APZHUB — **not** as an alternative brand for a file store or repository product.

It enables:

- document capture
- organisation
- discovery
- lifecycle
- governance
- compliance
- collaboration

through a **native APZHUB experience**. The implementation engine remains invisible.

Unlike Time or Support, Documents is inherently **cross-cutting**. It naturally supports APZ Projects, APZ Support, APZ Time, APZQEP, APZ Law Platform, and future products — as an enterprise capability delivered through a native product, not as a siloed repository destination.

| Audience                 | Role in the product                                                             |

| Knowledge workers        | Create, find, and update documents needed for daily work                        |
| Project / delivery teams | Attach and retrieve documents that belong to delivery context                   |
| Support & operations     | Reference procedures, evidence, and customer-facing records                     |
| Quality / APZQEP actors  | Relate documents to quality evidence by reference                               |
| Compliance / records     | Ensure retention, ownership, and access are governed                            |
| Leaders                  | Trust that critical documents are findable and permissioned without tool sprawl |

## Primary business problem

Documents fragment across inboxes, personal drives, chat attachments, and product-specific silos. That creates version confusion, weak access control, lost institutional knowledge, and slow decisions. APZ Documents exists to eliminate that fragmentation **in service of work** inside APZHUB.

## Product principles

1. **Business capability, not a file store** — Documents enables managed document work; it is not “APZHUB’s Paperless.”
2. **Cross-cutting by design** — serves every product that needs document context.
3. **SoR discipline** — owns document lifecycle and document metadata only; other products keep their SoR; link by reference ([SYSTEM-OF-RECORD.md](./SYSTEM-OF-RECORD.md)).
4. **Product-centric UX** — users attach, review, and sign in the context of work; engines stay invisible.
5. **Document context** — documents almost never exist in isolation ([DOCUMENT-CONTEXT.md](./DOCUMENT-CONTEXT.md)).
6. **One authoritative place per document** — versions and ownership are clear.
7. **Permissioned by default** — findability never overrides access control.
8. **Strong product boundaries** — Documents does document management; it is not the whole suite.
9. **Business language** — outcomes and metrics executives and users understand.

## Product promise

If a document belongs to APZHUB work, people will know where it lives, who owns it, which version is current, who may see it, and **what work it serves** — without ever thinking they are using a backend repository product.

## Long-term vision

APZ Documents becomes the trusted document plane of APZHUB: connective tissue across Projects, Time, and Support; foundation for Law and other document-heavy products; continuous improvement of document practice under APZQEP.

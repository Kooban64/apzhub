## Mission statement

> **APZ Documents gives every person in the organisation one trusted place inside APZHUB to create, organise, find, share, and govern the documents that work depends on — with clear ownership, permission, and connection to projects, support, and quality.**

## Product purpose

APZ Documents exists as the **document management capability** of APZHUB — not a side file store. It is where authoritative document metadata, versions, and discovery live so that people stop scattering critical records across email, chat, and unmanaged drives, and so that Projects, Support, Time, and quality work can attach to the same trusted documents.

## Primary users

| Audience                 | Role in the product                                                             |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Knowledge workers        | Create, find, and update documents needed for daily work                        |
| Project / delivery teams | Attach and retrieve documents that belong to delivery context                   |
| Support & operations     | Reference procedures, evidence, and customer-facing records                     |
| Compliance / records     | Ensure retention, ownership, and access are governed                            |
| Leaders                  | Trust that critical documents are findable and permissioned without tool sprawl |

## Primary business problem

Documents fragment across inboxes, personal drives, chat attachments, and product-specific silos. That creates version confusion, weak access control, lost institutional knowledge, and slow decisions. APZ Documents exists to eliminate that fragmentation inside APZHUB.

## Product principles

1. **One product experience** — users work in APZ Documents; they do not navigate a separate document world.
2. **One authoritative place per document** — versions and ownership are clear.
3. **Documents serve work** — connect to Projects, Support, and other products without absorbing them.
4. **Permissioned by default** — findability never overrides access control.
5. **Strong product boundaries** — Documents does document management; it is not the whole suite.
6. **Business language** — outcomes and metrics executives and users understand.
7. **Engines stay invisible** — users experience APZHUB, not backend brands.

## Product promise

If a document belongs in APZHUB work, people will know where it lives, who owns it, which version is current, and who may see it — and related products can attach to that document without forcing users into a fragmented file landscape.

## Long-term vision

APZ Documents becomes the trusted document plane of APZHUB: the place teams prefer for records that matter because it is clearer, safer, and more connected than unmanaged storage — and a natural attach point for Projects, Support, Workflow, Analytics, and Law.

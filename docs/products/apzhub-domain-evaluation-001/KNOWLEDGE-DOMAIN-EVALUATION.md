## Objective

Evaluate whether the Knowledge domain should become the next authorised business domain for APZHUB / APZOR.

This evaluation does **not** authorise expansion. It produces evidence for Owner decision.

---

## 1. Business problem

**What problem does Knowledge solve?**

APZOR already has Systems of Record for work, service, effort, documents, process intent, decisions, and governance obligations. What is still missing is a coherent enterprise answer to:

- What policy applies?
- What procedure should I follow?
- Have we solved this before?
- Where is the documentation?
- What lessons were learned?
- Which standards apply?

Documents preserves information artefacts. Law owns governance objects. Neither product’s identity is “institutional knowledge that makes every other product smarter in context.” Knowledge addresses that gap.

---

## 2. Immediate beneficiaries (existing products)

| Product       | How Knowledge would amplify (by reference — not redesign)         |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| APZ Projects  | Procedures, playbooks, prior delivery lessons in context          |
| APZ Support   | Solved-before knowledge, runbooks, escalation standards           |
| APZ Documents | Curated knowledge vs general document library (boundary required) |
| APZ Workflow  | Procedure / journey guidance linked to process stages             |
| APZ Analytics | Evidence and institutional context behind insight questions       |
| APZ Law       | Policy/procedure discoverability without becoming legal advice    |
| APZQEP        | Lessons learned / standards discoverability for quality work      |

Productivity Core and Governance Layer both gain. This is the primary cross-domain case.

---

## 3. Amplification of Enterprise Productivity Core

**Assessment: Strong.**

Knowledge does not replace Projects, Support, Time, Documents, Workflow, or Analytics. It increases their operating value by reducing rediscovery, inconsistent practice, and “tribal knowledge” risk — the same friction-reduction principle already frozen in the Enterprise Operating Model.

---

## 4. Strengthening of Enterprise Governance Layer

**Assessment: Meaningful, complementary.**

Law owns obligations, policies, retention, and evidence as governance objects. Knowledge can surface “what policy / procedure / standard applies here” in work context — by reference to Law and Documents — without becoming counsel or a legal research product.

Risk to manage at Mission time: do not collapse Law into Knowledge, or Knowledge into Law.

---

## 5. Foundation for future AI

**Assessment: Strategic enabler — not AI-first.**

A curated, permissioned knowledge domain creates the natural substrate for later AI assistance (retrieval, summarisation, suggested procedures) without placing AI at the centre of the platform or forcing AI into every RI.

AI remains Lane 1 / separate Owner Auth. Knowledge evaluation does not authorise AI programmes.

## 7. Risks and conditions (for Owner if authorising later)

1. **SoR boundary with Documents** must be Mission-defined before engineering.
2. **Governance boundary with Law** must remain consume-by-reference.
3. Prefer context-in-product experience over a standalone “knowledge portal” identity.
4. Do not treat advisory ranking as substitute for this scorecard (this programme exists to prevent that).

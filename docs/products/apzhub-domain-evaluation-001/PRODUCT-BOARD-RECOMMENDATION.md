## Recommendation

# RECOMMEND AUTHORISATION

## Statement

The Product Board recommends that the Owner **authorise** the Knowledge domain as the next enterprise expansion domain, subject to a separate Owner Authorisation that opens a Mission pack.

This recommendation is **not** Mission authorisation.  
This recommendation is **not** Native Adoption.  
**Do not create KNOWLEDGE-000 unless explicitly authorised by the Owner.**

## Why not DEFER

Deferral would be appropriate if readiness were weak, SoR collision unresolved with no path, or value speculative. Here:

- Baseline portfolio is complete and frozen for the core
- Cross-domain leverage is concrete and immediate
- Complexity is proportionate relative to Finance / heavy ERP-class domains
- Strategic fit with “context” as platform strength is clear

Residual Mission design (Documents boundary) is normal Mission work — not a reason to defer scoring-based recommendation.

## Why not DO NOT PROCEED

No criterion scored below 4. The domain is in the Domain Strategy as planned. Out-of-scope risks (wiki sprawl, replacing Documents, legal research) are controllable by Mission rules already foreshadowed in the Domain Catalogue.

## Conditions for Owner (if accepting)

1. Open a **Mission** programme only — no engineering in Auth.
2. Mission must define Information Domain placement and **Documents SoR boundary**.
3. Mission must keep Law as governance SoR; Knowledge consumes by reference.
4. Prefer governance-in-context / work-context experience over standalone portal identity.
5. AI remains out of Mission scope unless separately authorised.

## Rule

This ranking is **strategic guidance** for which candidates to score first.  
It is **not** mandatory.  
It does **not** authorise a Mission.

Authorisation requires a completed Evaluation Matrix scorecard and Owner Auth.

## Recommended evaluation order

Based on portfolio leverage after the Enterprise Portfolio Baseline — preferring domains that increase the value of what already exists before the most complex enterprise functions.

| Priority | Domain          | Reason                                                                                                                                                      |
| -------- | --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1        | **Knowledge**   | Amplifies every existing product by making enterprise knowledge searchable and reusable. Natural foundation for future AI without placing AI at the centre. |
| 2        | **People**      | Builds on Operations, Workflow, Documents and Governance to support the employee lifecycle.                                                                 |
| 3        | **Customer**    | Extends Projects, Support and Workflow into customer relationship management.                                                                               |
| 4        | **Risk**        | Complements Governance and Analytics with enterprise risk management.                                                                                       |
| 5        | **Procurement** | Natural workflow- and governance-driven business domain.                                                                                                    |
| 6        | **Assets**      | Operational asset lifecycle management.                                                                                                                     |
| 7        | **Finance**     | Largest scope and greatest integration complexity; best undertaken once the platform is mature.                                                             |

## Strategic note — why Knowledge first (advisory)

The platform’s greatest strength is now **context**. Knowledge unlocks questions such as:

- What policy applies?
- What procedure should I follow?
- Have we solved this before?
- Where is the documentation?
- What lessons were learned?
- Which standards apply?

That amplifies Projects, Support, Documents, Workflow, Analytics and Law simultaneously.

## Strategic note — why not Finance first (advisory)

Finance remains strategically important but high in integration complexity. Prefer domains that compound existing portfolio value before introducing the heaviest business functions.

## Established domains

Foundation · Quality · Operations · Business Process · Information · Insight · Governance are **not** selection candidates — they are already established in the baseline. Selection applies to **planned expansion** domains only.

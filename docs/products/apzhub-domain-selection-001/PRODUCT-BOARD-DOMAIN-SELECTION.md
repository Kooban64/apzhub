## Decision

> **The Portfolio Baseline is complete. Future investment shall begin with deliberate domain selection rather than sequential product expansion. Each new domain must demonstrate measurable enterprise value before authorisation.**

## Meaning

| From                                      | To                                                           |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Expanding because another product remains | Expanding because the **business chose** the next capability |
| Preference / engineering interest         | **Scored business value**                                    |
| Lane 2 paused indefinitely                | Lane 2 **READY** — after selection gate                      |
| Sequential product list                   | Evaluation Matrix → Owner Auth → Mission                     |

## Consequences

1. No expansion Mission without a completed Domain Evaluation Scorecard.
2. Advisory candidate ranking may guide evaluation order — it does not authorise.
3. Lane 1 remains **FROZEN** (evidence exceptions only).
4. Lane 2 is **READY** to open a domain **only** after scored selection + Owner Auth.
5. Playbook and architecture remain unchanged.
6. Knowledge is the **recommended first candidate to score** — not yet authorised.

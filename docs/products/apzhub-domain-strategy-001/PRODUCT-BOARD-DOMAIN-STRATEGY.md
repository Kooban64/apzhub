## Decision

> **The APZHUB Enterprise Portfolio Baseline is accepted as the canonical reference for the platform. Future investment decisions shall be made at the business domain level and implemented through the established Product Native Adoption Standard.**

## Meaning

| From                             | To                                                   |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Building APZHUB                  | **Growing APZHUB**                                   |
| “What product do we build next?” | **“What business capability does APZOR need next?”** |
| Product-centric roadmap          | **Domain-centric strategy**                          |
| Architecture as bottleneck       | **Domain choice as bottleneck**                      |

## Consequences

1. Enterprise Portfolio Baseline remains canonical for “what exists.”
2. Enterprise Domain Strategy becomes canonical for “where we may grow.”
3. No new product Mission opens without naming its **business domain**.
4. Native Adoption Playbook and APZQEP remain unchanged.
5. Lane 2 is **READY** after Domain Selection Method — Mission only after scored selection + Owner Auth.
6. Selection gate: [../apzhub-domain-selection-001/](../apzhub-domain-selection-001/).
7. APZ Law remains APZHUB-internal governance — not a commercial legal domain.

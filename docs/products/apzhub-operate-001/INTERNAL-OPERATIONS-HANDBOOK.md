## What APZHUB is (for operators)

APZHUB is the Enterprise Productivity Platform APZOR uses for daily work. Staff start in **My Work**, then act in the owning product.

| Ask                   | Answer                                              |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| What do I need to do? | **My Work** (`/workspace/home`)                     |
| How do I do it?       | Owning product (Projects / Support / Time / APZQEP) |

Engines (Plane, Zammad, Kimai, etc.) are **invisible**. Never train staff on engine UIs as the APZHUB path.

## Products in scope (internal rollout)

| Product          | RI                   | Primary use                                         |
| ---------------- | -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **APZ Projects** | #003                 | Delivery work, tasks, coordination                  |
| **APZ Support**  | #002                 | Service requests and resolution                     |
| **APZ Time**     | #001                 | Effort capture and accountability                   |
| **APZQEP**       | Baseline             | Quality / release discipline for engineering change |
| **My Work**      | Portfolio capability | Obligation queue across products                    |

## Daily operating posture

1. Sign in once to APZHUB.
2. Open **My Work**.
3. Act on Needs My Attention / Due Today.
4. Complete work in the owning product.
5. Return to My Work for the next obligation.

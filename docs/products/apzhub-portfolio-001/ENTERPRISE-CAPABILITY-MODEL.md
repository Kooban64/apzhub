## Purpose

Describe what **APZHUB delivers as an enterprise** — in business language.

> Not: “APZ Time owns Time Management.”  
> Instead: “APZHUB delivers Workforce Productivity through APZ Time.”

## Rules

1. Organised around **business capabilities**, not modules or engines.
2. APZHUB is the deliverer; products are the vehicles.
3. Engines remain invisible.
4. Does **not** authorise engineering or the next product programme alone.
5. Update when a product earns Reference Implementation status or Owner Auth changes priority.
6. Read with the [Enterprise Operating Model](./ENTERPRISE-OPERATING-MODEL.md).

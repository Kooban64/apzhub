## Statement

APZHUB products must feel like **one platform**, not a federation of tools.

These principles are **portfolio promises**. They do not authorise new programmes; they constrain how future work is judged.

## Permanent work principle (Phase 2)

> **APZHUB answers “What do I need to do?”**  
> **Products answer “How do I do it?”**

The platform composes obligations (My Work and related portfolio experiences). Products remain Systems of Record and the place of deep work. Composition never becomes a second SoR.

## Capability investment filter (frozen)

> **Every new capability must eliminate measurable operational friction, not simply add functionality.**

Elevated after APZHUB-CAPABILITY-001-ENG-001. See [ENTERPRISE-OPERATING-MODEL.md](./ENTERPRISE-OPERATING-MODEL.md) and [../framework/APZHUB-PLATFORM-PHASE-2-UNIFIED-EXPERIENCE.md](../framework/APZHUB-PLATFORM-PHASE-2-UNIFIED-EXPERIENCE.md).

## The seven platform promises

| #   | Promise                     | User-visible meaning                                     |
| --- | --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **One identity**            | Sign in once to APZHUB. No product-local authentication. |
| 2   | **One navigation**          | Same shell: Activity Bar, Sidebar, Workspace, Context.   |
| 3   | **One search**              | Search APZHUB; results respect permissions.              |
| 4   | **One notification centre** | Attention arrives through APZHUB, not product inboxes.   |
| 5   | **One workspace**           | Sessions, layout, and chrome are platform-owned.         |
| 6   | **One quality platform**    | Every change runs through APZQEP.                        |
| 7   | **One audit trail**         | Accountability is platform-owned and immutable.          |

## Cross-product experience principles

| Principle                       | Meaning                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Work vs how                     | APZHUB answers what needs doing; products answer how to do it                                               |
| Platform owns “My …”            | My Work, My Approvals, My Notifications, My Documents, My Projects, My Tickets, My Time are APZHUB surfaces |
| Products feed; they do not silo | Products contribute data and actions; they do not invent parallel shells                                    |
| Native language only            | Users see APZ Projects / APZ Time / APZ Support — never engines                                             |
| Same quality of experience      | Empty, loading, error, help, and settings patterns converge (Product Experience Convergence)                |
| Permissions are platform truth  | UI may hide; server remains authoritative                                                                   |

## Anti-principles

| Anti-pattern                          | Why it fails the platform story |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Second login or engine SSO screen     | Breaks one identity             |
| Product-only search console           | Breaks one search               |
| Per-product notification subsystem    | Breaks one attention model      |
| Adapter/ops console in product chrome | Breaks native experience        |
| Dual quality / release path           | Breaks one quality platform     |

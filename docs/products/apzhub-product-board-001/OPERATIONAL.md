## Rule

**Stream B (reactive):** No _adjustment / UX / evidence-driven_ engineering without an **accepted** friction record.

**Stream A (planned):** Capability Evolution Roadmap investments are strategic and **do not** require a friction entry — they still require Owner Auth per programme.

## Product Board Operations (ACTIVE)

Review cadence (Stream B):

1. Review newly recorded frictions.
2. Validate supporting evidence.
3. Eliminate items solvable without engineering.
4. Select at most one reactive capability for investment.
5. Authorise APZQEP engineering only after Owner approval.

Planned waves: choose via Capability Evolution Roadmap with the Product Board — separate from this register.

## Contract

Every APZ Knowledge change follows APZQEP:

1. Open a Quality Flow
2. Classify change / identify components
3. Evaluate impact and policies
4. Produce Decision Package
5. Capture Evidence
6. Complete Engineering / Quality / Release checklists
7. Record Operational Learning when something hurt or taught

No parallel quality process. No bypass for “small” memory UX changes.

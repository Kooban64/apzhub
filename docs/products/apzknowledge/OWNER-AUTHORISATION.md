## Strategic objective

Define the mission of APZ Knowledge as the enterprise organisational memory of APZHUB.

This programme defines **why the product exists**, not how it will be implemented.

## Governing Product Board principles (immutable)

1. APZ Knowledge is the enterprise organisational memory.
2. The right knowledge appears in the right place at the right time.
3. Knowledge is derived from trusted enterprise experience. It is not created independently of it.
4. Knowledge without context is information. Knowledge in context becomes organisational capability.
5. Knowledge never becomes a System of Record for operational truth.
6. Knowledge references trusted Systems of Record. It does not replace them.

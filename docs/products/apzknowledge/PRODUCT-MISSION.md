## Purpose

APZ Knowledge exists so the enterprise can **remember what it has learned** and apply that memory where work happens — without inventing truth or replacing Systems of Record.

It answers one portfolio question:

> **What do I need to know to do this well?**

## Vision

> **The right knowledge appears in the right place at the right time.**

See [PRODUCT-VISION.md](./PRODUCT-VISION.md).

## Enterprise role

APZ Knowledge is the **enterprise organisational memory** of APZHUB.

| Layer                   | Products                                                     | Knowledge role                                                             |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Productivity Core       | Time · Support · Projects · Documents · Workflow · Analytics | Consumes memory in context; sources lessons and practice                   |
| Governance              | Law                                                          | Consumes / contributes governance interpretation — Law owns artefacts      |
| Quality                 | APZQEP                                                       | Sources engineering standards and approved practice — APZQEP owns evidence |
| Information (expansion) | **Knowledge**                                                | Owns curated organisational memory only                                    |

Knowledge is connective tissue: it makes every product smarter without becoming their System of Record.

## Business identity

> **APZ Knowledge is the enterprise product that captures, curates, contextualises and delivers organisational memory to help people make better decisions and perform work correctly.**

| APZ Knowledge **is**                                       | APZ Knowledge is **not**     |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Enterprise organisational memory                           | A document repository        |
| Memory Companion (understanding in context)                | A wiki                       |
| Curated lessons, standards, procedures, decision rationale | A search engine              |
| Derived from trusted enterprise experience                 | An AI assistant              |
| Consumed by every product                                  | A RAG platform               |
| References to trusted SoRs                                 | A learning management system |

Those excluded forms may consume or contribute knowledge later; they do **not** define the product.

## Value proposition

See [VALUE-PROPOSITION.md](./VALUE-PROPOSITION.md).

In one line: **Documents store information; Knowledge delivers understanding in context.**

## Mission statement

> **APZ Knowledge is the enterprise organisational memory of APZHUB — curated understanding derived from trusted enterprise experience, surfacing the right knowledge in the right place at the right time, without replacing any System of Record.**

## Governing principles (immutable)

1. **Organisational memory** — Knowledge owns memory only.
2. **Right place, right time** — permanent product vision.
3. **Derivation** — from trusted enterprise experience; not created independently of it.
4. **Context** — without context = information; in context = organisational capability.
5. **Never operational SoR** — references trusted SoRs; does not replace them.
6. **Engines invisible** — no vendor or AI identity as the product.

## Portfolio separation (protected)

| **Knowledge** | **Organisational memory**             |

## Primary business problem

Without APZ Knowledge, organisational understanding lives in people, chat, and scattered files. Products execute work but do not systematically answer _what do I need to know to do this well?_ Rediscovery, repeated mistakes, and knowledge loss become structural.

## Product promise

If someone is doing work in APZHUB and needs trusted organisational understanding, APZ Knowledge can surface the right memory in context — without pretending to own projects, documents, policies, workflows, analytics, or AI.

## Long-term vision

APZ Knowledge becomes the connective tissue of APZHUB — not by storing everything, but by helping every product answer what must be known to act correctly, while protecting every System of Record.

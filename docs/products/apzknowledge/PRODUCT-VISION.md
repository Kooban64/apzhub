## Permanent vision

> **The right knowledge appears in the right place at the right time.**

## Experience model

```text
Work → Relevant Memory → Correct Action
```

Primary experience: memory in the context of work already underway.  
Secondary experience: browse and curate organisational memory.

## Strategic prediction (identity guardrail)

APZ Knowledge becomes the **connective tissue** of the platform — not because it stores everything, but because it helps every product answer:

> **What do I need to know to do this well?**

If that identity is protected, Knowledge will not become another repository. It will make the rest of APZHUB smarter without replacing Systems of Record.

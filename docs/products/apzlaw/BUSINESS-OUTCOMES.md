Outcomes are organisational governance results. They are not feature inventories.

| ID    | Outcome                                                       | Why it matters                                                           |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| BO-01 | Leaders can ask “are we allowed?” inside APZHUB               | Governance stops living only in email and tribal knowledge               |
| BO-02 | Applicable obligations are visible before action              | Reduces accidental non-compliance in operational products                |
| BO-03 | Policies that govern actions are identifiable                 | Clear ownership of which policy applies                                  |
| BO-04 | Required evidence retention is explicit                       | Audit and regulatory readiness without ad-hoc folders                    |
| BO-05 | Compliance artefacts are governed, not scattered              | Artefacts remain findable and attributable                               |
| BO-06 | Reviews are coordinated without replacing counsel             | Legal/compliance collaboration with clear product boundaries             |
| BO-07 | Regulatory relevance can be surfaced for a context            | Users know which requirements may apply — without automated legal advice |
| BO-08 | Governance does not collapse into Documents or Workflow       | Product identity and SoR boundaries preserved                            |
| BO-09 | Convenience never overrides governance UX                     | Compliance Before Convenience remains enforceable                        |
| BO-10 | Productivity Core remains free of counsel-substitution claims | Law owns governance; other products stay operational / insight focused   |

## Outcome → portfolio value

| Business value (portfolio) | Outcomes                   |

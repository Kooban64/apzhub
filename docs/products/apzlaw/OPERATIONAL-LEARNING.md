## Law-specific learning themes

- Did users start from governance questions or from practice administration?
- Did practice-first vocabulary re-enter the standard UX?
- Did practice surfaces remain secondary?
- Was deferred consumer wiring mis-classified as a Law defect?
- Did anyone request law-firm / commercial legal SaaS expansion — and was it refused?
- Did Law stay as APZHUB-internal governance (not external legal profession tooling)?

## Anti-patterns

- Skipping learning because “it was only docs”
- Opening Lane 1 or practice programmes from a single friction note
- Treating missing matter/trust/billing features as Native Adoption failure
- Collapsing Law into a practice management console “for power users”
- Growing Law toward standalone legal SaaS without Owner Auth — permanently forbidden

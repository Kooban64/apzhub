## Permanent product identity

APZ Law is **an internal governance product for APZHUB only**.

It is **not** a legal practice management system, case management system, law firm product, or commercial legal SaaS.

It is:

> **The governance capability of the APZHUB Enterprise Platform.**

Once Native Adoption is complete, there is **no future ambition** to grow APZ Law into a standalone legal platform. It grows only as APZHUB's governance requirements grow.

## Principle 1

> **APZ Law governs APZHUB.**

It does not govern the external legal profession.

## Principle 2

> **APZ Law provides governance context to APZHUB products.**

It does not replace legal advice.

## Principle 3

> **Governance is experienced where work happens.**

APZ Law owns governance.  
Projects, Workflow, Documents, Support, APZQEP and future products consume governance by reference.

## Principle 4

> **APZ Law exists only because APZHUB exists.**

Its roadmap is driven solely by APZHUB governance requirements.

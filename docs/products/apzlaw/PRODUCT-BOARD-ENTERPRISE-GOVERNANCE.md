## Context

Phase 2 of the APZHUB portfolio — the **Enterprise Productivity Core** (RI #001–#006) — is complete. APZ Law opens the **Enterprise Governance Layer**.

## Enterprise Governance Principle

> **Law governs business obligations. It does not provide legal advice.**

| The product **does**        | The product **does not**              |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Surface obligations         | Replace legal counsel                 |
| Manage compliance artefacts | Advise on how to win a case           |
| Track policies              | Practice law as a firm                |
| Coordinate reviews          | Guarantee regulatory outcomes         |
| Retain governance evidence  | Act as a lawyer or substitute for one |

## Compliance Before Convenience

> **Where governance and convenience conflict, governance defines the product.**

UX shortcuts that weaken obligation visibility, evidence retention, or policy control fail this principle — even if they feel faster.

## Governance Context Principle

> **Governance should appear where work is performed, not only inside the Law product.**

| Law owns                                                                                    | Other products consume by reference      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Governance knowledge and lifecycle (policies, obligations, compliance artefacts, retention) | Applicable policies on a Project         |
|                                                                                             | Required approvals on a Workflow         |
|                                                                                             | Regulatory obligations on a Support case |
|                                                                                             | Retention requirements on a Document     |
|                                                                                             | Compliance evidence in APZQEP            |
|                                                                                             | Governance trends in Analytics           |

This mirrors Documents and Analytics: a dedicated product owns the domain; everyday work surfaces consume it **by reference**. Law must not become the only place governance is visible — nor absorb operational SoRs.

## Product identity (standing)

> **APZ Law is the enterprise product that governs policies, obligations, compliance, and legal artefacts across APZHUB.**

| APZ Law **is not** (alone) | May become capabilities later, but do not define identity |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| A document repository      | Documents remain APZ Documents for information management |
| A contract system          | Contracts may be artefacts under governance               |
| A legal database           | Searchable law is not the product metaphor                |
| A case management tool     | Cases may be a governed workflow, not the whole product   |

## Questions APZ Law answers

- Are we allowed to do this?
- What obligations apply?
- Which policies govern this action?
- What evidence must be retained?
- Which regulatory requirements are relevant?

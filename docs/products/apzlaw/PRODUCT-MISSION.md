## Mission statement

> **APZ Law is the enterprise governance product of APZHUB — the place that governs policies, obligations, compliance, and legal artefacts across the platform, without providing legal advice or becoming a substitute for counsel.**

## Product identity

| APZ Law **is**                                        | APZ Law is **not**              |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Enterprise governance & compliance capability         | A document repository           |
| The product that answers “are we allowed?”            | A contract system alone         |
| Policies · obligations · compliance · legal artefacts | A legal database                |
| Governance evidence retention                         | A case-management product alone |
| Coordinator of reviews and policy application         | Legal advice or counsel         |

**Law asks:** _Are we allowed to do this — and what must we retain?_  
**Productivity Core asks:** _What work exists, how does it move, and what should we decide?_

Keep those questions separate.

## Product purpose

APZ Law exists as the **Enterprise Governance** capability of APZHUB.

It governs. It does **not** own:

- project plans (APZ Projects)
- service requests (APZ Support)
- time records (APZ Time)
- general document lifecycle as information management (APZ Documents)
- business process definitions as intent (APZ Workflow)
- enterprise performance insight (APZ Analytics)
- quality evidence / release decisions (APZQEP)

Those remain their Systems of Record. Law **coordinates obligations and governance artefacts** that apply across them. Legal artefacts may reference Documents; they do not turn Law into Documents.

## Governing principles

1. **Law governs business obligations. It does not provide legal advice.**
2. **Where governance and convenience conflict, governance defines the product.**
3. Law surfaces obligations, policies, compliance artefacts, reviews, and retained evidence.
4. Engines and external counsel tools stay invisible as product identity.
5. Strong boundaries with Documents, Workflow, Analytics, and the Productivity Core.

## Primary users

| Audience            | Role in the product                                             |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Compliance leads    | Track obligations, policies, and evidence retention             |
| Legal / counsel     | Coordinate reviews; Law does not replace their advice           |
| Risk / governance   | See which policies govern actions across APZHUB                 |
| Product / ops leads | Ask “are we allowed?” before acting in other products           |
| Executives          | Assure governance posture without practising law in the product |
| Operators / admins  | Administer policy catalogues and retention settings             |

## Primary business problem

The Enterprise Productivity Core lets the organisation execute, support, document, orchestrate, and decide — but does not yet answer, as a first-class product, whether actions are allowed, which obligations apply, which policies govern, and what evidence must be retained. Without APZ Law, governance fragments across Documents, ad-hoc folders, and counsel email — or gets confused with legal advice software.

## Product principles

1. **Obligations first** — scope starts with governance questions, not contract templates.
2. **Governance before convenience** — UX yields to obligation clarity and evidence.
3. **Not counsel** — never position the product as legal advice.
4. **Artefacts under governance** — contracts, policies, and evidence are governed objects, not the whole identity.
5. **Cross-product applicability** — obligations may attach to Projects, Support, Workflow, etc.
6. **Native APZHUB experience** — users never navigate a separate “legal engine” world.
7. **Engines stay invisible** — no vendor branding as product identity.
8. **Strong boundaries** — Law does not absorb Documents, Workflow, or Analytics.
9. **Governance layer** — sits beside the Productivity Core; does not redefine it.

## Product promise

If an organisation has a governance or compliance question that APZHUB should answer — _are we allowed, what applies, what must we retain?_ — they can pursue it through APZ Law without mistaking the product for a lawyer.

## Long-term vision

APZ Law becomes the trusted governance layer across APZHUB — policies, obligations, compliance, and legal artefacts — while remaining an enterprise product that never pretends to practise law.

## Vision

APZHUB becomes an enterprise operating platform where **operations**, **decision support**, and **governance** coexist:

| Layer                           | Role                                                          |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Enterprise Productivity Core    | Execute, support, deliver, document, orchestrate, decide      |
| **Enterprise Governance Layer** | **Govern obligations, policies, compliance, legal artefacts** |
| Future Expansion                | HR, Finance, CRM, Procurement — plug into the core            |

APZ Law is the first product of the Governance Layer.

## Experience intent

Users enter through **governance questions**, not through a contract vault or case list as the product metaphor:

- Are we allowed to do this?
- What obligations apply?
- Which policies govern this action?
- What evidence must be retained?
- Which regulatory requirements are relevant?

## Non-vision

APZ Law does not become “ChatGPT for lawyers,” a litigation suite, or a replacement for external counsel. Contract repositories, case tools, and legal databases may appear as capabilities later — they never redefine the product.

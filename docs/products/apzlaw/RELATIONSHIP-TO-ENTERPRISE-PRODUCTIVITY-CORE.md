## Layer distinction

```text
Enterprise Productivity Core (RI #001–#006)
        │  do the work / understand the work
        ▼
APZ Law — Enterprise Governance Layer
        │  are we allowed? what must we retain?
        ▼
Compliant action + retained evidence
```

Law **plugs into** the core. It does not redefine Time, Support, Projects, Documents, Workflow, or Analytics.

## Strategic implication

The Governance Layer is **OPERATIONAL** (RI #007). It is capability expansion after Phase 2 — not foundation completion and not a Playbook redesign. APZ Law does not evolve into a standalone legal platform.

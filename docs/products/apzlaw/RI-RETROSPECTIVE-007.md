## What the Playbook proved again

- N-01…N-04 works for a **governance-layer** product
- Identity leakage (practice management as product) is correctable without Playbook redesign
- Permanent product boundary decisions belong in Product Board principles, not in feature backlog

## Distinctive lesson

APZ Law’s hardest correction was not presentation or execution leakage — it was **wrong product ambition**. Clarifying that Law is APZHUB-internal governance (not a law-firm product) prevented a permanent architectural mistake.

## Patterns to observe (not promote yet)

- Governance Companion (above) / Practice tooling (below)
- Work → Governance Context → Confident Action
- Consume-by-reference into Productivity Core products

## Do not do next

- Reopen Native Adoption to add matters/trust/billing as identity
- Start a commercial legal SaaS programme under the Law brand
- Treat missing practice features as defects

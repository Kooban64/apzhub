# APZ Law — Value Proposition

| Field     | Value            |
| --------- | ---------------- |
| Programme | APZ-LAW-000      |
| Status    | **APPROVED**     |
| Timestamp | 20260805T185100Z |

## For the enterprise

One governed place in APZHUB to understand **policies, obligations, compliance, and legal artefacts** — without fragmenting governance across Documents folders, Workflow workarounds, and counsel inboxes.

## For leaders

Confidence that “are we allowed?” and “what must we retain?” are first-class platform questions — not afterthoughts.

## For counsel and compliance

A collaboration surface for obligations, reviews, and evidence — that **does not pretend to be the lawyer**.

## For the Productivity Core

Operational products stay focused on work; Analytics stays focused on decisions; Law attaches governance without becoming those products.

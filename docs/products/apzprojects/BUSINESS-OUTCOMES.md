Outcomes are organisational results. They are not feature lists.

| ID    | Outcome                                  | Why it matters                                                |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| BO-01 | Clearer shared plans                     | Teams align on what will be delivered and why                 |
| BO-02 | Stronger ownership of work               | Tasks and outcomes have accountable owners                    |
| BO-03 | Faster identification of delivery risk   | Blockers and slip surface early enough to act                 |
| BO-04 | Better cross-team coordination           | Handoffs and dependencies are visible, not tribal             |
| BO-05 | Higher on-time delivery confidence       | Commitments are honest and tracked                            |
| BO-06 | Reduced status-chasing overhead          | Less time spent reconstructing progress outside the product   |
| BO-07 | Better portfolio visibility              | Leaders see what is in flight without spreadsheet archaeology |
| BO-08 | Clearer connection of work to outcomes   | Effort ties to goals, releases, and value                     |
| BO-09 | Stronger foundation for related products | Time, Support, Documents, and quality have a shared centre    |
| BO-10 | Measurable delivery improvement          | Decisions rest on evidence, not anecdote                      |

## Outcome → portfolio value

| Business value (portfolio)  | Outcomes                   |

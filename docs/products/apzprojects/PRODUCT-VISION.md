# APZ Projects — Product Vision

| Field     | Value            |
| --------- | ---------------- |
| Programme | APZ-PROJECTS-000 |
| Status    | **COMPLETE**     |
| Timestamp | 20260805T052000Z |

## Near term (post mission agreement)

APZ Projects is the unmistakable APZHUB place for planning and coordinating delivery: clear ownership, honest progress, and mission-aligned metrics guiding improvement.

## Medium term

Delivery coordination becomes a managed organisational capability: dependencies are visible, risk surfaces early, leaders act on evidence, and related APZHUB products attach to shared project context when authorised.

## Longer term

APZ Projects is the operational backbone of APZHUB daily work: teams prefer it over informal workarounds; Time, Support, Documents, quality, Workflow, and Analytics enrich a shared centre; continuous improvement of delivery is routine.

## Vision constraints

- Remain a **projects / delivery coordination** product — do not become Support, Time, Documents, Workflow, or Analytics.
- Remain an **APZHUB** product experience — not a separate project world.
- Expand capability only when operational evidence and Owner Authorisation justify it.
- Do not absorb APZQEP; keep quality infrastructure separate while enabling richer context later.

## What Makes This Different

Most products establish governance after they have accumulated complexity.

APZQEP established governance **before** large-scale expansion.

Every future capability therefore inherits:

- consistent architecture,
- consistent documentation,
- consistent engineering,
- consistent certification,
- and consistent release governance.

That posture is intended to reduce technical debt and improve maintainability as the platform grows.

The **APZQEP Foundation Programme** is complete in every material respect.

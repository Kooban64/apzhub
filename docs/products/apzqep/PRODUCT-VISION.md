# APZ QEP — Product Vision

> **Programme:** APZQEP-TRANSITION-001  
> **Official name:** APZ QEP — APZ Quality Engineering Platform  
> **Former name:** APZ TCMS (retained in historical documentation)

## Vision statement

**APZ QEP is an Enterprise Quality Engineering Platform.**

Its purpose is to govern software quality throughout the **entire software delivery lifecycle**.

Testing is **one capability** of the platform — not its identity.

## Purpose

The platform shall manage the complete quality lifecycle, including:

- Requirements (quality-relevant)
- Verification (manual, automated, AI-assisted, continuous)
- Traceability
- Automation orchestration (not replacing runners)
- AI-assisted quality engineering
- Evidence management
- Release readiness
- Certification
- Continuous quality improvement

## Strategic positioning

| Was (TCMS)                  | Is (QEP)                                    |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Test Case Management System | Quality Engineering Platform                |
| Test cases as centre        | Verification as centre (broader than cases) |
| Testing product             | SDLC quality governance product             |
| AI deferred / assistive     | AI-native assistants; QEP remains SoR       |

## Users

QA leaders, verification analysts, automation engineers, developers, release managers, auditors, compliance, product owners, executives, and external enterprise customers.

## Platform relationship

Native APZHUB product on **Certified Platform 1.4**. Extends the platform; does not redesign it. Module → Platform Service → Connector → Engine remains mandatory.

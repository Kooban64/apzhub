(e.g. “build OpenAI”). After a month of use it may instead be:

- a better release summariser;
- historical weighting for impact correlation;
- Playwright flaky-test awareness;

…or something else entirely. Authorise what **reality** demands under
[APZQEP-CHANGE-CONTROL.md](../v1.1/apzqep-version-1.1-architecture-freeze/APZQEP-CHANGE-CONTROL.md).

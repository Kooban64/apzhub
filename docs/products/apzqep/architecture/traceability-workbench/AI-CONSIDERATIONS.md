# AI Considerations — APZQEP-ARCH-008

> Companion extract. Authoritative detail: [TRACEABILITY-WORKBENCH-ARCHITECTURE.md](./TRACEABILITY-WORKBENCH-ARCHITECTURE.md) §21. Aligns with ARCH-007 AI considerations and ENG-030A Part 1 AI-promotion policy.

## AI as consumer

AI may consume Trace Links, search projections, and future Coverage / Impact analysis results to assist users.

## AI never owns

| Forbidden | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Trace Link SoR | Domain authority |
| Business rules / lifecycle | Part 1 domain |
| Authority / auto-approval | Governance |
| Silent confidence elevation | Confidence is governed |

## Workbench implication

Future UI may surface `ai_suggestion` origin proposals in Validation queues. Promotion requires human or policy-controlled workflows before authoritative states. **No AI implementation under ARCH-008.**

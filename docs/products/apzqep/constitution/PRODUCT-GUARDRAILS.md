# APZ QEP — Product Guardrails

> **Programme:** APZQEP-CONSTITUTION-001  
> **Authority:** Constitutional (Article VIII)

## Scope — what APZ QEP is

APZ QEP remains focused on **Enterprise Quality Engineering**:

- Quality-relevant requirements
- Verification (manual, automated, AI-assisted, continuous)
- Traceability, evidence, defects, risk
- Release readiness and continuous certification
- Quality analytics/intelligence and knowledge
- Governed AI assistants and MCP/IDE adjacency
- Enterprise governance on APZHUB Platform

## Scope — what APZ QEP shall not become

| Guardrail                                            | Statement                                                                                     |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Do not become an ALM platform**                    | Do not replace Projects/Jira/Linear as the system of work management                          |
| **Do not become a CI/CD platform**                   | Do not own pipelines, runners orchestration as a CI product, or SCM admin UX                  |
| **Do not become a device cloud**                     | Do not compete with BrowserStack-class device/browser farms                                   |
| **Do not compete with automation runners**           | Do not replace Playwright, Cypress, JUnit, Vitest, etc.                                       |
| **Remain focused on Enterprise Quality Engineering** | Reject feature requests that dilute category identity into generic TCMS clone or DevOps suite |

## Product behaviour guardrails

1. Prefer verification language over test-case-only identity.
2. Prefer certification and evidence depth over vanity feature breadth.
3. Prefer governed AI over flashy ungoverned AI.
4. Prefer platform suite adjacency over reinventing Platform capabilities (Identity, Notify, Search, Documents, Observability).
5. Prefer honest limitations over overselling.
6. Prefer self-hosted / CE-first options for enterprise trust.

## Competitive guardrails

- Do not define success as feature parity with TestRail/Xray/Zephyr.
- Do not adopt Kiwi TCMS as SoR or user-facing product.
- Optional ALM sync is allowed; ALM ownership is not.

## Violation handling

Product proposals that violate these guardrails require Owner Decision to amend this Constitution — not silent scope creep in Definition or Engineering.

# APZ QEP — Discovery Summary

> **Programme:** APZQEP-DISCOVERY-001  
> **Audience:** Owner · Product Definition authors

## One-page synthesis

**Category choice:** Enterprise Quality Engineering Platform — not a TestRail clone, not a Jira plugin, not a runner, not a device cloud.

**Market gap:** Continuous certification + evidence-first SoR + governed AI/MCP on a suite platform is under-served; TCMS AI features are common but weakly governed.

**Attack surface:** Certification readiness, evidence, platform governance, MCP-native IDE agents, verification maturity, executive quality intelligence.

**Table stakes:** Still must cover commodity TCMS capabilities for credibility.

**AI stance:** AI-native vision; runtime default OFF; interchangeable providers; humans accountable; never SoR; never auto-certify.

**MCP stance:** Preferred IDE/agent channel through Gateway/Authz; QEP remains SoR; no autonomous certify tools.

**Principles:** Quality before testing; evidence before opinion; certification before release; everything traceable, auditable, measurable.

**Next:** On Discovery Acceptance → **APZQEP-DEF-001** Product Definition Baseline, guided by this pack + REQ-001.

## Decisions Discovery recommends for Definition

1. Lead messaging with QE + certification, not “test cases.”
2. Scope MVP = commodity + competitive + flagship differentiator slices.
3. Sequence AI UX after AI governance plumbing.
4. Treat MCP as a first-class client class in Definition.
5. Keep Kiwi and runners out of product identity.
6. Cite PRODUCT-PRINCIPLES as permanent constraints.

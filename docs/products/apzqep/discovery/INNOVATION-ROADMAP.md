## Innovation rules

1. No horizon authorises Engineering alone.
2. Each AI enablement requires named Owner programme.
3. Certification firewall never relaxes across horizons.
4. Commodity parity may ship in H0 without claiming innovation.
5. Prefer depth in flagship differentiators over breadth of copycat features.

## Discovery mandate

1. Understand the Quality Engineering / test management market.
2. Identify industry best practice and enterprise expectations.
3. Map competitor strengths and weaknesses honestly.
4. Establish where APZ QEP must **intentionally differentiate**.
5. Surface innovation opportunities (AI, MCP, continuous certification).
6. Produce permanent product principles and a long-term strategy that guide Definition.

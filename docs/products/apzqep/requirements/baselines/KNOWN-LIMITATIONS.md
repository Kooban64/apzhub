Authorised scope decisions (not defects):

- No unlock, restore, clone, merge, or branching
- No baseline deletion through ordinary product functionality
- No cross-project or mixed-domain baselines

- No downstream Verification / Execution / Evidence consumption yet

search publication is eventually consistent.

# APZQEP Product Positioning

| Field         | Value                                            |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Status        | **IN FORCE** (Product Board guidance 2026-08-01) |
| Official name | **APZ QEP** — APZ Quality Engineering Platform   |
| Positioning   | **Enterprise Quality Engineering Platform**      |

---

## Decision

APZQEP is **not** positioned internally or externally as a traditional “Testing Platform” or test-case manager alone.

Use:

```text
Enterprise Quality Engineering Platform
```

This reflects governed requirements → verification → evidence → release confidence, Quality Intelligence, AI assistance under human authority, and portfolio-scale quality operations — beyond classical TCMS scope.

Existing product definition already states this ambition ([product-definition](../product-definition/README.md)). New and revised documents **SHOULD** prefer “Enterprise Quality Engineering Platform” / “Quality Engineering” over “Testing Platform” shorthand.

## Consequences for design

- Prefer platform services, evidence, certification, and release-confidence language in architecture and UX.
- Avoid designing features that only make sense inside a narrow test-repository product.
- AI, QI, and executive surfaces remain quality-engineering capabilities — not optional add-ons to a test tool.

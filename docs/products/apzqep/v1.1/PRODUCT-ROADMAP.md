## Vision

APZQEP becomes the **operating system for quality engineering** at APZOR: every requirement, test, execution, defect, and evidence artefact is discoverable, governed, AI-assisted, and release-aware — without abandoning the v1.0 foundation.

**Positioning (not copy):** Tuskr/Testmo simplicity · TestRail/Xray depth · Azure Plans enterprise workflow · qTest scale — expressed as APZHUB-native modules on the existing platform.

## Principles

1. **Preserve v1.0** — frozen domains change only via explicit Owner enhancement programmes
2. **Operate before decorate** — Runs/Suites/Defects/Search before vanity analytics
3. **AI assists; humans decide** — no autonomous release/certification actions
4. **Limited Availability honesty** — do not claim GA until Owner gate
5. **Programme discreteness** — independently closable deliveries

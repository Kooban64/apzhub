# Defect Management

Enterprise Defect Management is the authoritative source for quality findings investigation and resolution.

| Concern                    | Owner                    | Mutability                          |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| What happened              | Cap C Execution Results  | Immutable (governed amendment only) |
| Supporting artefacts       | Evidence Platform        | Immutable refs                      |
| Investigation & resolution | **Capability D Defects** | Governed lifecycle                  |

Defects may be created manually or raised from failed/blocked execution steps. Execution history is never modified by defect operations.

| Field     | Value            |

## Strategic intent

Make APZQEP the enterprise quality operating system: useful from day one via automation orchestration, competitive via CI/CD integrations, differentiated via governed AI quality intelligence, and sticky via executive dashboards and continuous quality loops — all on APZHUB.

## Product principles (mandatory)

1. Evidence-first engineering
2. Governed AI (advisory; humans certify)
3. Enterprise governance preserved
4. Certification before release
5. Operations-led evolution
6. Platform neutrality (runner/provider abstraction)
7. Backward compatibility with Version 1.0 where practical
8. Architecture-first planning
9. No speculative engineering
10. Module → Platform Service → Connector → Engine (no bypass)

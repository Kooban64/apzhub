## Differentiation

APZQEP’s Dashboard & Quality Experience differentiates from generic enterprise quality / ALM dashboards by:

1. **Governed quality** — permission-driven, audited, Zero Trust end-to-end.
2. **Explainability** — every recommendation exposes why, evidence, confidence, provider.
3. **Evidence-first** — screenshots, video, traces and relationships are first-class, not attachments afterthoughts.
4. **Quality Intelligence visualisation** — observations → signals → recommendations → scores as a coherent story.
5. **Executive decision support** — release readiness with human certification authority retained.
6. **Provider-neutral platforms underneath** — Automation, SCM, QI are swappable; dashboards do not lock customers to a vendor AI or SCM.

## What we refuse commercially

- Opaque AI scoreboards
- Dashboard-as-SoR
- Mandatory Enterprise Edition engine dependencies
- Chatbot theatre without evidence and audit

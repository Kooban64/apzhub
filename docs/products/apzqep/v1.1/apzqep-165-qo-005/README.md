## Mission

Provider-neutral Impact Correlation Engine: analyse normalized changes, correlate enterprise quality assets, produce an explainable impact graph, calculate confidence and risk, and recommend an advisory quality scope.

**Never decides what to execute.**

## Guiding principle

> The Impact Correlation Engine produces an explainable impact graph and confidence model. It never decides what to execute.

# Explainability

Every bundle explains residual governance state.

Mandatory explanation surface:

- required authorities
- authority assignments (from template snapshot)
- decisions (authority, state, actor context, timestamp)
- comments, conditions, exceptions
- outstanding approvals
- delegated approvals
- escalations
- residual / final approval status

Explainability is produced from the immutable bundle — never by re-evaluating policies, gates, or evidence.

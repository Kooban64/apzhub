# Residual Risk

Residual risk is **composed** from prior engines — no new risk math.

| Field                          | Composition                                     |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Residual Risk Level            | Max of impact risk and governance residual risk |
| Contributing Factors           | Impact factors + status markers                 |
| Outstanding Governance Items   | From governance snapshot                        |
| Outstanding Approvals          | From approval snapshot                          |
| Outstanding Quality Activities | From create input (already selected upstream)   |

Approval rejected/exceptions may elevate composed residual risk via max-level composition only.

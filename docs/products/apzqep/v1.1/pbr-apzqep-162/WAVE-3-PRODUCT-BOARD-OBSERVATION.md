## Observation

APZQEP-160 currently titles Wave 3 / APZQEP-163 as **AI Quality Intelligence**.

Product Board observation (recommended):

```text
Refine the Wave 3 programme title to:

Quality Intelligence Platform

with AI as one governed capability/provider inside it —
not the architectural centre.
```

Rationale:

1. Waves 1–2 established peer platform packages (`platform-automation`, `platform-scm`) with provider-neutral cores.
2. Centring Wave 3 on “AI” risks model-vendor coupling and understates risk/impact/scoring/readiness value.
3. APZQEP-160’s capability model (Stream C) already describes broader intelligence outcomes (clustering, release advisor, insights) that fit a Quality Intelligence Platform framing.
4. This observation does **not** amend APZQEP-160 text; Owner Authorisation for APZQEP-163 may adopt the refined title.

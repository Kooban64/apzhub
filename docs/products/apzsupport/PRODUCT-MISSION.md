## Mission statement

> **APZ Support gives every person in the organisation one clear place inside APZHUB to ask for help, follow progress, communicate, and reach resolution — quickly, visibly, and with confidence.**

## Product purpose

APZ Support exists so that requesting help and delivering service are first-class APZHUB experiences: consistent with how people already work in the platform, accountable to service commitments, and free of tool sprawl or fragmented handoffs.

## Primary users

| Audience         | Role in the product                                                        |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Requesters       | Staff (and, where enabled, customers) who need help                        |
| Support agents   | People who triage, respond, and resolve                                    |
| Service managers | People who own quality, capacity, and SLAs                                 |
| Leaders          | People who need operational visibility without living in day-to-day queues |

## Primary business problem

Today, help often fragments across channels, tools, and informal workarounds. That slows first response, weakens ownership, hides backlog risk, and makes service quality hard to measure. APZ Support exists to eliminate that fragmentation inside APZHUB.

## Product principles

1. **One product experience** — users work in APZ Support; they do not navigate a separate support world.
2. **Clarity over clutter** — every request has an owner, a status, and a next step.
3. **Speed with accountability** — fast acknowledgement and resolution, measured honestly.
4. **Visible quality** — managers see service health without chasing people or spreadsheets.
5. **Strong product boundaries** — APZ Support does support; it does not become the whole enterprise suite.
6. **Business language** — outcomes and metrics executives and users understand.

## Product promise

If you raise a request in APZ Support, you will know it was received, who owns it, where it stands, and when it is resolved — and the organisation will be able to prove how well that promise is kept.

## Long-term vision

APZ Support becomes the trusted service hub of APZHUB: the place people prefer for help because it is faster, clearer, and more reliable than email, chat sprawl, or side channels — and the place leaders trust for service quality and continuous improvement.

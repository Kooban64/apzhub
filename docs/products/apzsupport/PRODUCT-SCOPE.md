APZ Support **owns** the following business capabilities (product sense — not implementation):

| Capability                  | Meaning                                                                     |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Request / ticket management | Capture, classify, prioritise, and lifecycle-manage support requests        |
| Communication               | Structured dialogue between requesters and support staff about a request    |
| Work assignment             | Clear ownership and routing of work to the right people or teams            |
| Knowledge access            | Find and use approved answers and guidance while helping or self-serving    |
| Operational visibility      | See backlog, status, age, and service health for management of support work |
| Service quality             | Track and improve how well support commitments and outcomes are met         |
| Escalation management       | Move work to higher ownership when first-line resolution is insufficient    |
| Closure & feedback          | Resolve requests cleanly and learn from requester feedback                  |

Scope is expressed as **what the product is responsible for**, not how it is built.

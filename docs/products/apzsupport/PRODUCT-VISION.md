# APZ Support — Product Vision

| Field     | Value            |
| --------- | ---------------- |
| Programme | APZ-SUPPORT-000  |
| Status    | **COMPLETE**     |
| Timestamp | 20260805T040600Z |

## Near term (post mission agreement)

APZ Support is the unmistakable APZHUB place for help: request, communicate, resolve, and see status — with mission-aligned metrics guiding improvement.

## Medium term

Support quality becomes a managed organisational capability: workload is balanced, SLAs are honest, knowledge reduces repeat effort, and leaders act on evidence rather than anecdote.

## Longer term

APZ Support is indispensable to daily work: people prefer it over informal channels because it is faster and clearer; service performance is a standing leadership conversation; continuous improvement is routine.

## Vision constraints

- Remain a **support** product — do not become projects, time, documents, or CRM.
- Remain an **APZHUB** product experience — not a separate support world.
- Expand capability only when operational evidence and Owner Authorisation justify it.

| Stakeholder                            | Expectations                                                  | Responsibilities                                                                                                |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Internal requesters**                | A single, clear place to ask for help and see progress        | Raise clear requests; respond to clarifying questions; accept or reopen outcomes honestly                       |
| **External customers** (where enabled) | Professional, timely communication without tool confusion     | Same as requesters within the access they are given                                                             |
| **Support agents**                     | Manageable queues, clear ownership, enough context to resolve | Triage, communicate, resolve, document outcomes; escalate only when needed                                      |
| **Service / support managers**         | Visibility of quality, capacity, and SLA risk                 | Set priorities; coach quality; balance workload; act on metrics                                                 |
| **Product Owner (APZ Support)**        | Mission stays true; scope stays bounded                       | Protect outcomes and boundaries; refuse work that does not serve the mission                                    |
| **Engineering**                        | Clear mission and metrics before build                        | Implement only under later authorised programmes; follow APZHUB quality practice when engineering is authorised |
| **Executives / Product Board**         | Service quality and operational risk are visible              | Approve portfolio priority; accept material product direction; fund the right outcomes                          |
| **Operations**                         | Stable day-to-day service operation                           | Care for production readiness of the product experience once adoption programmes are authorised                 |

## Stakeholder promise

APZ Support is judged by whether these people experience clearer ownership, faster help, and better visibility — not by how many features exist on a roadmap.

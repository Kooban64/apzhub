## What is delivered

Permissioned users can capture and manage timesheets and related records in `/workspace/time` on a durable fail-closed adapter-backed path, with honest limitation disclosure and Delivery-Standard hardening.

## Honest limitations (v1.0)

- Approvals, reporting UI, analytics, dashboards, leave, scheduling, and AI are **out of product identity**.
- Tags search may be **partial**.
- Production forbids in-memory domain mode — unavailable storage **fails closed**.
- Health and diagnostics are **operator surfaces**.

## Out of scope

Time 2.0, redesign, architecture reopen without ADR, Platform Evolution (blocked until Portfolio Completion).

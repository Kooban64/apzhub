| Field     | Value            |

| Status    | **APPROVED**     |

Outcomes are organisational results. They are not feature lists.

| ID    | Outcome                                    | Why it matters                                                      |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| BO-01 | Governed cross-product journeys            | Handoffs stop living only in tribal knowledge                       |
| BO-02 | Clear next-step ownership                  | People know who acts next and why                                   |
| BO-03 | Faster, more predictable handoffs          | Less waiting between Projects, Support, Time, Documents, quality    |
| BO-04 | Visible journey status                     | Leaders and teams see where work sits in the journey                |
| BO-05 | Consistent use of the operational backbone | RI products are used as designed, not bypassed                      |
| BO-06 | Separation of intent from execution        | Automation remains an implementation concern, not the product face  |
| BO-07 | Reduced coordination overhead              | Less status-chasing across tools and channels                       |
| BO-08 | Auditability of journey progress           | Organisation can show that governed steps were followed             |
| BO-09 | Measurable orchestration performance       | Improvement decisions rest on evidence                              |
| BO-10 | Stronger foundation for Analytics and Law  | Later products consume journey context without redesigning Workflow |

| Business value (portfolio) | Outcomes                   |

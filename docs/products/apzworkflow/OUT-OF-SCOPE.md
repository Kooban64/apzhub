## Boundary rules

1. Workflow **models, governs, and visualises business processes**; it does not absorb other products’ SoRs.
2. **Automation is execution** — out of product identity for this mission.
3. Failures of the [Workflow Test](./WORKFLOW-TEST.md) are out of product vocabulary.
4. “Helpful while we’re here” expansions that cross these boundaries require separate Owner Authorisation.
5. Lane 1 platform evolution remains evidence-driven — not justified by opening Workflow.

# APZ Workflow — Product Vision

| Field     | Value            |
| --------- | ---------------- |

| Status    | **APPROVED**     |

## Near term (post mission agreement)

APZ Workflow is the unmistakable APZHUB place for governed cross-product journeys: clear steps, clear ownership, and mission-aligned metrics — never an exposed automation engine.

## Medium term

Priority organisational journeys (for example project approval → work → documents → time → quality) are encoded once and followed consistently. Informal bypass declines.

## Longer term

Together with the four Reference Implementations, APZ Workflow completes the operating rhythm of APZHUB. Analytics and Law integrate around journey context. Continuous improvement of journeys is routine.

## Vision constraints

- Remain an **orchestration** product — do not become Projects, Support, Time, Documents, or APZQEP.
- Remain **intent**, not automation branding.
- Expand capability only with Owner Authorisation.
- Do not open Lane 1 platform programmes from this vision alone.

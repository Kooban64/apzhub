# APZ Workflow Version 1.0 — User Guide

## What APZ Workflow is

APZ Workflow is the **business process companion** — define and follow organisational journeys (stages, ownership, outcomes). Automation engines stay out of sight.

## Getting started

1. Open **APZ Workflow** in the Activity Bar.
2. Use **Open journey catalogue** or **Journeys** to browse business journeys.
3. Open a journey to see stages, governance status, and related activity.
4. Use **Template library** for reusable process templates.
5. Use **Process monitoring** for instance progress signals.

## What you will not see

- Engine product branding (e.g. n8n) as the primary experience
- A working **Start run** control unless your organisation later unlocks provider execute under separate authority

## Approvals

Approvals and participants appear under the Workflow product. From Projects, some approvals may show as unavailable if the automation path is gated — contact your administrator.

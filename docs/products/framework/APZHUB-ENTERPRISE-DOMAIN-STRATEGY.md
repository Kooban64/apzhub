## Purpose

Define the enterprise **business domains** APZHUB intends to own over the next 3–5 years.

Not products.  
Domains.

Investment decisions shall ask:

> **What business capability does APZOR need next?**

Products implement domains through the established Product Native Adoption Standard. Domains do not authorise engineering by themselves.

## Governing decision

> **The APZHUB Enterprise Portfolio Baseline is accepted as the canonical reference for the platform. Future investment decisions shall be made at the business domain level and implemented through the established Product Native Adoption Standard.**

This marks the transition from **building APZHUB** to **growing APZHUB**.

## Rules

1. Domains are **business-owned**; products are delivery vehicles.
2. A domain may contain **zero, one, or many** products.
3. Existing RI products are placed into domains — identities are not redesigned.
4. Future products emerge from domain need → Mission → Native Adoption → optional RI.
5. Engines remain invisible.
6. Lane 1 (platform evolution) remains evidence-driven.
7. Lane 2 is **READY** for expansion only after Domain Selection Method scoring + Owner Auth.
8. This strategy does **not** schedule delivery or invent products.
9. Selection gate: [APZHUB-DOMAIN-SELECTION-METHOD.md](./APZHUB-DOMAIN-SELECTION-METHOD.md).

## How domains relate to layers and products

```text
Business Domain  →  Enterprise Layer  →  Native Product(s)  →  Engine (invisible)
```

| Ask                                      | Do not ask first                                  |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Which domain needs maturity?             | Which app should we build?                        |
| What capabilities belong in that domain? | Which engine should we integrate?                 |
| Which Mission should open?               | How do we stretch an existing product’s identity? |

## Expansion path (Lane 2 READY — selection gate first)

```text
Score candidate domain(s) on Evaluation Matrix
        →
Product Board records scores + rationale
        →
Owner Authorisation names selected domain
        →
Mission pack for the chosen capability / product
        →
Owner Approval
        →
Native Adoption (N-01…N-04) under APZQEP
        →
Optional RI designation
        →
Update Domain Catalogue · Product Catalogue · RI map
```

No domain in “Planned expansion” is authorised until the selection gate and Owner Auth complete.

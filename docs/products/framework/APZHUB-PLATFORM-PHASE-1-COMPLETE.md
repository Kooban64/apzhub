## Horizons (post Phase 1)

| Horizon | Focus                   | Change posture                                       |
| ------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **1**   | Enterprise Platform     | **Frozen** — changes only through governance         |
| **2**   | Core Business Products  | Continuous maturation under Playbook + APZQEP        |

Horizon 1 examples: identity, APZQEP, Integration SDK, Native Adoption Standard, Enterprise Capability Model.  
Horizon 2 examples: Projects, Time, Support, Documents, Workflow, Analytics, Law Platform.  
Horizon 3 examples: Unified Work Experience, unified notifications, executive workspace, productivity intelligence — when justified.

## Methodology pause

No further standards, playbooks, or governance packs are required to “finish building the platform.”

Every future investment discussion starts with:

> **Which business capability creates the greatest operational improvement over the next quarter?**

The methodology executes. It does not reinvent itself.

## Commercial identity

> **APZHUB is an Enterprise Productivity Platform that delivers integrated business capabilities through a portfolio of native products governed by a common quality, identity, operational, and engineering model.**

Implementation engines remain invisible infrastructure.

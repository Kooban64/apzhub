## Declaration

> **Unified Experience is enabled.**  
> Products are native. A portfolio capability exists. Work is organised around the user rather than the applications.

This is the point from which APZHUB is no longer only a portfolio of excellent products — it has a **native portfolio capability** that sits above those products without taking ownership away from them.

## What Phase 2 adds (beyond Phase 1)

| Phase 1                           | Phase 2                                            |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Operating model established       | First Horizon 3 capability **composed in product** |
| Three Reference Implementations   | Unified Work Experience Composition Layer live     |
| Methodology frozen                | Work-first landing: **My Work**                    |
| “How does APZHUB make us better?” | “What do I need to do?” is answerable in APZHUB    |

## Permanent platform principle (elevated)

> **APZHUB answers “What do I need to do?”**  
> **Products answer “How do I do it?”**

Composition presents obligations. Products remain Systems of Record and the place of deep work. Cards are references — never a second SoR.

See also: [../apzhub-portfolio-001/PLATFORM-EXPERIENCE-PRINCIPLES.md](../apzhub-portfolio-001/PLATFORM-EXPERIENCE-PRINCIPLES.md).

## Commercial identity (Phase 2)

> **APZHUB is an Enterprise Productivity Platform that presents work—not applications—to its users. Native products remain the authoritative owners of business data, while the platform composes a unified work experience, common identity, common quality model, and shared operational standards.**

Implementation engines remain invisible infrastructure.

## Operational posture (mandatory)

**No new Horizon 3 capability programme** until My Work has been used in production long enough to produce meaningful operational learning.

Do **not** immediately authorise:

- Unified Notifications
- Unified Search
- Executive Workspace

Next investment is decided by evidence from observation, not engineering momentum.

The governing question is no longer “What capability should we build next?”

It is:

> **What did My Work fail to answer?**

Companion:

> **What caused a user to leave My Work?**

Leaving for detailed work in an owning product is healthy. Leaving because a portfolio capability is missing is evidence.

| Observed failure                      | Candidate response (only if evidenced) |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Users missed important items          | Notifications                          |
| Users could not find work             | Search                                 |
| Executives wanted summaries           | Executive Workspace                    |
| Managers wanted approvals aggregation | Approval Lens                          |

## Success criterion

Not “users opened My Work.”

Success is:

> **Users stopped thinking about which product to open.**

They start in My Work and complete work that may span Projects, Support, Time, and APZQEP without consciously switching mental context. Measured via the [Work Completion Journey](./APZHUB-WORK-COMPLETION-JOURNEY.md); reviewed in the [My Work Review](./APZHUB-MY-WORK-REVIEW.md).

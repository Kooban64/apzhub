## Rules

1. One product Mission / Native Adoption track at a time (unless Owner Auth says otherwise).
2. Mission packs: **no engineering, no architecture, no engine names**.
3. Lane 1 (platform evolution) remains evidence-driven.
4. Lane 2 is **READY** — Domain Selection Method + Owner Auth required before any expansion Mission.
5. Updating this roadmap is a Product Board / Owner act — not an engineering initiative.
6. APZ Law identity is **APZHUB-internal enterprise governance** — not legal practice / advice / commercial legal SaaS.
7. Future products must complete Native Adoption before joining the operational platform map.
8. Expansion starts from the Enterprise Portfolio Baseline and Domain Strategy — not from rediscovering product packs.
9. Investment decisions are made at the **business domain** level, then implemented through Native Adoption.

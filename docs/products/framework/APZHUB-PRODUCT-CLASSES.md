## Gate for future domains

> **Does this introduce a genuinely new enterprise capability, or does it simply duplicate one we already have?**

If it duplicates, do not open a new Mission. Extend or consume the existing capability.

**Enterprise Capability First** (stronger Product Era rule):

> **No new domain shall be authorised while an existing enterprise capability remains materially incomplete for its intended users.**

See [APZHUB-ENTERPRISE-CAPABILITY-FIRST.md](./APZHUB-ENTERPRISE-CAPABILITY-FIRST.md).

## Purpose

APZHUB is managed as an **enterprise portfolio**, not a sequence of engineering programmes.

Once per quarter (or when Owner convenes an ad-hoc review), answer only:

1. **Which product will create the biggest operational improvement?**
2. **What did operational learning from existing Reference Implementations teach us?**
3. **Does any evidence justify evolving the Playbook or APZQEP?**

If the answer to (3) is **no**, standards remain unchanged.

## Rules

- Do not open multiple Native Adoption programmes in parallel without explicit Owner Auth.
- Do not auto-select the next product from roadmap order.
- Do not redesign the Playbook from preference.
- Record the decision — even when the decision is “continue operating RIs; no new Native Adoption this quarter.”

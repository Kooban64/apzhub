## Decision

The finite inventory for Platform Engine Foundation v1.0 is accepted as contractual scope.

Programme 001 is **consolidation / elevation / certification** of existing platform capabilities into **APZ Platform Engines (APE)** — not invention of seven products, not AI, not product redesign.

## Purpose

Create the official **APZHUB Requirements Engineering Standard** — the **mandatory first activity** for every new product, module, enhancement, or major change.

No Product Definition may commence until requirements for that change are captured, analysed, prioritised, and approved under this standard (after Owner Acceptance of this programme).

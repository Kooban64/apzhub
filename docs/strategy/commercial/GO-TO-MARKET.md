## Internal adoption strategy

1. Designate internal Product/Service Owners per Enterprise Operating Model.
2. Run Production on Platform **1.1.0** with real Support/Projects/Time/Law paths.
3. Capture onboarding friction → Training + Implementation Methodology.
4. Do not wait for Email SoR / execute unlock to adopt certified surfaces.

## External customer strategy

1. Qualify against ICP and Known Limitations disclosure.
2. Preferself-hosted CE engines; mask brands in demos.
3. Sell Professional with Standard support; Enterprise for SSO/governance depth.
4. Explicit non-sell: Workflow execute, Email SoR, Support webhooks/realtime, FIN-001.

## Competitive positioning

| Vs                                    | Position                                                  |
| ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Point tools (Jira/Zammad/Kimai alone) | Unified Workbench + identity + permissions                |
| Big suites (ServiceNow etc.)          | Self-hosted, modular, OSS-backed, lower lock-in narrative |
| Raw OSS installs                      | Brand-masked, platform AuthZ, ops/governance packaged     |

Never compete by claiming uncertified automation/execute depth.

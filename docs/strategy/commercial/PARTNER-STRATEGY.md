# APZHUB Partner Strategy

> **Programme:** APZHUB-STRATEGY-001  
> **Date:** 2026-07-20  
> **Editions:** Partner / OEM

---

## Partner types

| Type           | Role                                                    |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Referral       | Introduces logos; APZOR delivers                        |
| Delivery / MSP | Implements and supports under Partner edition           |
| OEM            | Embeds APZHUB under partner brand (Presentation Engine) |
| Technology     | Engine/hosting alliances (non-exclusive)                |

## Partner economics (principles)

- Margin-compatible packaging; numbers out-of-band
- Partners must complete enablement (TRAINING) before Production delivery
- Partners inherit KL honesty obligations

## Guardrails

1. No engine brand marketing in partner-facing end-user UIs.
2. No EE engine mandates.
3. Partner cannot grant STOP capabilities.
4. Security/compliance remains customer + APZOR joint responsibility.

> **Rule:** **No numeric prices** in this repository  
> **Authority principles:** [product-management/PRICING-STRATEGY.md](../../product-management/PRICING-STRATEGY.md)

---

## Execution principles

1. Price outcomes and editions — not engine brands.
2. PRWL products include limitation disclosure in quotes.
3. Suite bundles discount vs à-la-carte — Owner sets numbers out-of-band.
4. Law vertical may carry primary SKU premium.
5. Professional Services and Premium Support are separate line items.
6. Community is acquisition / evaluation — not unpaid Production SLAs.

## Subscription strategy

| Element   | Approach                                                                 |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Term      | Annual preferred; monthly for pilots                                     |
| Renewal   | Tied to edition + support tier                                           |
| Expansion | Module add-ons when certified                                            |
| Metering  | Seats/org optional — entitlement enforcement **not implemented** in-repo |

## What not to price as GA

Workflow execute · Email/SMS/push delivery · Support realtime · FIN-001 · uncertified AI.

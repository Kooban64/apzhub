## Competitive positioning (risk lens)

Position on **unified self-hosted Workbench + platform SoR honesty**, not on feature parity with SaaS suites that include Email/accounting/workflow execute. Competitive losses on those axes are expected until certified.

## Residual risk

Commercial strategy cannot eliminate engineering gaps; it contains them via packaging and sales discipline.

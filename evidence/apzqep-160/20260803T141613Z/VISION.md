## Vision statement

**APZQEP is the operating system for software quality.**

It is not a test-management application. It is the Enterprise Quality Hub that connects requirements, traceability, test design, automated and manual execution, evidence, AI quality intelligence, release readiness, and portfolio governance into one governed platform.

```text
Requirements → Traceability → Test Design → Automated & Manual Execution
        → Evidence Collection → AI Quality Intelligence
        → Release Readiness Score → Enterprise Dashboards & Reporting
```
